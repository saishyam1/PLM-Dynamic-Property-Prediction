Protein Tissue Enrichment Prediction

Multi-label tissue enrichment classifier benchmarking Ankh, ESM2, and ProtT5 protein language models across 235 tissues and a focused 5-label task.

Task design: Protein representations are extracted from pre-computed pooled embeddings (both max-pool and attention-pool variants). A shared-backbone MLP (dim→512→256→num_tissues) is trained with class-weighted BCE loss. For the focused 5-label task, a lighter FocusedMLP (dim→128→5) is trained with label smoothing, AdamW, and early stopping.

Preprocessing in this study
Downloaded the gene-attribute matrix (218 tissues × 14,532 genes). Mapped Ensembl protein IDs to UniProt accessions, removed tissues with <10 positive proteins, yielding 235 tissue labels × 13,081 proteins for model training.

Ankh 768-dim
Ankh-base protein LM. Local embeddings stored at ankh_embedding_cache/pooled_embeddings.npz. Training: 40 epochs, batch 64, lr 1e-3, cosine LR decay.
ESM2 1280-dim
ESM2-650M (fair-esm). Embeddings from esm2_embedding_cache/pooled_embeddings.npz on NFS. Training: 40 epochs, batch 64, lr 1e-3, cosine LR decay.
ProtT5 1024-dim
ProtT5-XL-UniRef50. Embeddings from protT5_embedding_cache/pooled_embeddings.npz on NFS. Training: 40 epochs, batch 64, lr 1e-3, cosine LR decay.
